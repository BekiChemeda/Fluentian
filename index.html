The Language Learning Problem

Why most learners struggle to reach fluency.

Language Barrier

Many platforms assume strong English proficiency, creating a massive barrier for non-native English speakers learning French.

Lack of Practice

Learners often lack conversation partners, making it difficult to develop real speaking confidence outside of textbooks.

Generic Paths

Most tools do not adapt learning paths to real objectives such as travel, education, or professional opportunities.

Exam Prep Struggles

Students preparing for official exams like DELF struggle to find realistic practice environments and mock tests.

The Fluentian Solution
