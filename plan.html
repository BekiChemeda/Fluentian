Frequently Asked Questions

How do the tutor payments work?

All payments are processed securely. Teachers are notified after payment and are paid only after you complete the session.

Do I need a subscription to buy a voice pass?

No! Add-ons like voice passes and 1-on-1 tutors are pay-as-you-go and do not require an active Pro or Pro Plus subscription.

Can I switch plans later?

Absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from your account settings.

What is the platform fee for tutors?

We charge a 15% service fee on tutor bookings to maintain the platform and ensure high-quality connections, applied after any bulk discounts.
